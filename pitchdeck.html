The Problem

Mobile Crypto Payments
Are Broken

$200B+ in mobile crypto volume with a UX worse than cash

QR Code Friction

Open camera, scan code, verify address, enter amount, confirm — 4+ steps just to send $5.

Wallet Pop-up Fatigue

Every single transaction needs a manual wallet approval. Buying coffee shouldn't feel like signing a mortgage.

No Privacy

All transaction history is public. The merchant sees your full wallet, every past payment, and your total balance.

No Risk Analysis

Send to the wrong address and funds are gone forever. No warning, no analysis, no safety net.

The Solution

Tap. Review. Done.

NFC reads the address. AI analyzes the risk. Vault signs instantly.

NFC Tap-to-Pay

One tap reads or writes Solana addresses. Phone-to-phone via HCE, or tap any NFC tag. No QR codes.

AI Guardian

5-check risk engine + LLM-powered summary before every payment. Green/yellow/red scoring. Auto-approve for trusted tiers.

Tap Vault

Pre-loaded local keypair stored in secure enclave. Payments sign locally — no MWA popup, sub-second confirmation.

Key Features

Feature Deep Dive

Ghost Mode

Privacy via fresh escrow PDA per payment. Recipient's wallet never appears in sender's history. Claim via any Blink client.

SKR Tiers

Ghost / Bronze / Silver / Gold. Higher tiers unlock bigger vault limits, better cashback, and auto-approve on green risk.

Yield Vault

Idle USDC (vault + unclaimed ghost) earns ~7% APY via Kamino lending. Shared PDA vault — no per-user rent. Yield funds SKR cashback, making the entire system self-sustaining.

AI Route Optimization

Before signing, an agent evaluates Jupiter swap vs. direct USDC transfer and picks the cheaper path automatically.

Solana Actions / Blinks

Full Actions spec v2.1.3. Ghost recipients claim from dial.to, Phantom, or Backpack — no PhasmaPay install needed.
